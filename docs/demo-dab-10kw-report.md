### Limitations

- `dc_bias_ignored`: iGSE ignores DC bias: only dB/dt and the peak-to-peak flux density enter the loss integral; the loss change under premagnetisation is not modelled.
- `no_minor_loops`: Minor loops are not decomposed: waveforms with more than two dB/dt reversals per period are rejected (policy reject) or evaluated with the major-loop peak-to-peak flux density applied to every segment (policy warn), which overestimates the loop-decomposed iGSE result when beta > alpha and underestimates it when beta < alpha.
- `no_relaxation`: No relaxation term (i2GSE): losses during zero-dB/dt intervals after a flux transition are not modelled.
- `sinusoidal_parameter_fit`: (k, alpha, beta) are a sinusoidal fit; the waveform generalisation is the iGSE hypothesis, validated in the literature for ferrites to within typically tens of percent, not an exact law.
- `temperature_specific_parameters`: Parameters apply at their fit temperature; no temperature correction is applied.
- `fit_range_only`: Extrapolation outside the fitted frequency / flux-density range is not validated; such inputs are rejected or flagged according to the domain policy.
- `noisy_sampled_data`: Sampled waveforms are used as given: measurement noise inflates |dB/dt|^alpha and can be counted as minor loops. Smooth or decimate measured data before evaluation; the reversal_tolerance argument only affects the loop count, never the loss integral.
- `litz_approximation`: Litz AC resistance uses Dowell's model with bundle_layers*sqrt(strands) equivalent strand layers and an estimated porosity; strand twisting/transposition effects are not modelled.
- `dowell_1d`: Dowell 1-D model: sinusoidal current at the fundamental, layers spanning the window, no gap fringing, no harmonic content, no termination effects.
- `no_thermal_model`: Conductor temperature is an input; no thermal model couples losses to temperature.
- `analytical_design`: Analytical sizing only: no finite-element, leakage-inductance, fringing, thermal, insulation or manufacturing analysis was performed.
- `no_hardware_validation`: No hardware was built or measured; results are design estimates, not qualification data.
- `effective_parameters`: Core effective parameters (Ae, le, Ve, window, mean turn length) are datasheet values; gapping, stacking and tolerances are not considered.
- `candidate_enumeration`: Candidate cores are enumerated from the catalogue (every core offered in the material) and ranked by total estimated loss; no area-product or thermal pre-selection is applied.

### Limitations

- `dc_bias_ignored`: iGSE ignores DC bias: only dB/dt and the peak-to-peak flux density enter the loss integral; the loss change under premagnetisation is not modelled.
- `no_minor_loops`: Minor loops are not decomposed: waveforms with more than two dB/dt reversals per period are rejected (policy reject) or evaluated with the major-loop peak-to-peak flux density applied to every segment (policy warn), which overestimates the loop-decomposed iGSE result when beta > alpha and underestimates it when beta < alpha.
- `no_relaxation`: No relaxation term (i2GSE): losses during zero-dB/dt intervals after a flux transition are not modelled.
- `sinusoidal_parameter_fit`: (k, alpha, beta) are a sinusoidal fit; the waveform generalisation is the iGSE hypothesis, validated in the literature for ferrites to within typically tens of percent, not an exact law.
- `temperature_specific_parameters`: Parameters apply at their fit temperature; no temperature correction is applied.
- `fit_range_only`: Extrapolation outside the fitted frequency / flux-density range is not validated; such inputs are rejected or flagged according to the domain policy.
- `noisy_sampled_data`: Sampled waveforms are used as given: measurement noise inflates |dB/dt|^alpha and can be counted as minor loops. Smooth or decimate measured data before evaluation; the reversal_tolerance argument only affects the loop count, never the loss integral.
